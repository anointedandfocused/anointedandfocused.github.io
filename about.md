I started Anointed and Focused because I believe real transformation begins with Christ and reaches into every area of life. Spiritual growth, disciplined living, wise stewardship, and purposeful work are all part of honoring Him, and I'm learning to walk that out one day at a time.
{: .lead}

## <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-seedling"></use></svg> Why I Write Here

<div class="card" markdown="1">

I write because I'm still being shaped. I don't have everything figured out, and I'm not writing from the finish line. I'm writing from the middle of the walk, where faith meets a Monday morning, a budget, a hard decision, or a habit I'm trying to change.

This blog is how I put what I'm learning into words, keep myself honest, and share it in case it helps you take your next step toward Christ.

**Faith. Discipline. Growth. Overflow. In that order.** Faith comes first because everything else depends on it. Discipline, growth, and overflow only mean something when they flow from Him.

</div>

## <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-book-open"></use></svg> What I Believe

<div class="card" markdown="1">

### Faith Comes First

Everything begins with God. Before goals, before success, before influence, I need to seek His direction. Faith is not an accessory to my life. It is the foundation I build upon.

### Discipline Builds Character

Growth rarely happens by accident. Daily obedience, consistent habits, and faithful stewardship shape who I become. Discipline isn't punishment. It's preparation for the purpose God has given me.

### Stewardship Is Worship

Every resource God places in my hands matters. My time, talents, finances, relationships, health, and influence are gifts to be managed with wisdom and gratitude.

### Purpose Over Popularity

I would rather be faithful than famous. I believe lasting impact comes from obedience to God, not from chasing recognition from people.

### Growth Never Stops

Becoming more like Christ is a lifelong journey. Every season offers a chance to grow in wisdom, humility, courage, and love, and I'm learning that I'm never finished.

### Overflow Is Meant to Be Shared

God's blessings were never meant to end with me. As I grow, I'm called to encourage others, serve generously, and leave every person better than I found them.

</div>

## <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-compass"></use></svg> Who This Is For

<div class="card" markdown="1">

This isn't about having all the answers. It's about being willing to grow.

I write for people who want to live their faith with intention, pursue excellence with humility, and honor God in every area of life.

### If you're ready to...

- Deepen your relationship with Christ.
- Build habits that reflect discipline instead of distraction.
- Lead yourself well before leading others.
- Become a faithful steward of your time, health, finances, and gifts.

## <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-lightbulb"></use></svg> My Values

<div class="card" markdown="1">

### Truth

God's Word is my foundation. It shapes how I think, how I live, and how I lead. Truth doesn't change with culture, and neither should my commitment to it.

### Faith

Every decision begins with trusting God. Prayer isn't my last resort. It's my first response. I want to choose obedience even when the path isn't easy.

### Discipline

Consistency matters. Small acts of faithfulness repeated over time shape my character far more than occasional moments of motivation.

### Stewardship

I want to honor God by managing my time, finances, talents, health, relationships, and influence with wisdom. Everything I have has been entrusted to me for His glory.

### Excellence

I pursue excellence not to impress people, but to honor God. Every opportunity deserves my best because my work reflects the One I serve.

### Community

Growth happens best together. I want to encourage you, be sharpened by you, and celebrate every step of progress with you. None of us is meant to walk this journey alone.

</div>

## <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-scroll"></use></svg> My Creed

<div class="card creed" markdown="1">

I believe faith is more than belief. It is a way of life.

I choose purpose over comfort, character over compromise, and obedience over convenience.

I pursue Christ before success, wisdom before wealth, and integrity before recognition. I steward every opportunity, every relationship, every resource, and every gift with gratitude.

I refuse to settle for a life of distraction when God has called me to live with purpose. I want to encourage the discouraged, strengthen the weary, celebrate the victories of others, and serve wherever God leads.

I am committed to becoming who God created me to be, so that everything I do brings glory to Him.

Faith.<br>Discipline.<br>Growth.<br>Overflow.
{: .creed-close}

</div>

## <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-mountain"></use></svg> My Mission

<div class="card" markdown="1">

I write to help people grow in their faith, strengthen their character, and faithfully steward every area of their lives for the glory of God.

I believe spiritual maturity should shape the way I work, lead, serve, manage money, care for my family, and pursue my purpose.

My desire is to help equip ordinary people to live extraordinary lives through faithful obedience to Christ.

This isn't about building a brand. It's about building people, starting with the one writing this, and pointing every one of us back to Christ.

</div>
