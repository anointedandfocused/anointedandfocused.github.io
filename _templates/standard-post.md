Write your opening paragraph here. Start with the heart of the post. A blank line starts a new paragraph.

## A main heading

Use `##` for main sections and `###` for smaller sections under them. Do not use a single `#`; the title above already does that job.

### A smaller heading

Write in plain text. Use **two asterisks for bold** and *one asterisk for italics*.

## Adding Scripture

Put a `>` at the start of each line. Put the reference inside `<cite>` on the last line. Include the Bible translation.

> "Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding."
>
> <cite>Proverbs 3:5 (ESV)</cite>

You can also mention a verse inside a sentence, like Philippians 4:13, with no special formatting.

## Lists, links, and images

- Add as many lines as you need.
