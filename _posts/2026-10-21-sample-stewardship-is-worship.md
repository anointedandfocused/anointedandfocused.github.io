Money shows me what I really trust. I can say that God is my provider, but my spending, saving, and giving tell the real story.

That is why I no longer see money management as a purely practical chore. I see it as worship. It is one of the ordinary ways I answer the question, *Do I believe all of this is His?*

## It all belongs to Him

Everything starts with ownership. I'm not the owner of my income, my home, or my savings. I'm a steward, a manager entrusted with something that belongs to someone else.

> "The earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof, the world and those who dwell therein."
>
> <cite>Psalm 24:1 (ESV)</cite>

That one shift changes the questions I ask. Instead of "What do I want to do with my money?" I ask, "What would a faithful manager do with what God has given me?"

## Four principles I'm working to live by

### 1. Plan with diligence

A plan is not a lack of faith. It is one of the ways I express faith.

> "The plans of the diligent lead surely to abundance, but everyone who is hasty comes only to poverty."
>
> <cite>Proverbs 21:5 (ESV)</cite>

For me that means writing down where my money goes each month before the month begins.

### 2. Build slowly

> "Wealth gained hastily will dwindle, but whoever gathers little by little will increase it."
>
> <cite>Proverbs 13:11 (ESV)</cite>

Quick fixes and get-rich promises are loud. Faithful, steady saving is quiet, and it works.

### 3. Treat debt seriously

Scripture is blunt about this one: "the borrower is the slave of the lender" (Proverbs 22:7). I'm not saying every loan is sinful. I am saying debt limits my freedom to obey when God calls me to give, to move, or to serve.

### 4. Hold it with an open hand

> "Do not lay up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust destroy and where thieves break in and steal, but lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven... For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."
>
> <cite>Matthew 6:19-21 (ESV)</cite>

Generosity is how I keep my heart from being captured by what I own.

## A starting point for you

- Write down what you earn and where it goes this month.
- Decide on your giving first, not with whatever is left over.
- Pick one small financial habit and keep it for 30 days.

You can read the full passages on [Bible Gateway](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+21&version=ESV).

*This is what Scripture is teaching me, not personalized financial advice. For decisions about your own situation, talk with someone you trust who understands your circumstances.*
