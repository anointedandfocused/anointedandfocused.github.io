## Why I do a weekly reset

Before the week speeds up, I try to slow down for a few minutes. I read a short passage, ask God what He wants me to notice, and choose one thing to act on. This video walks through that rhythm using a passage I come back to often.

## The passage

> "Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths."
>
> <cite>Proverbs 3:5-6 (ESV)</cite>

## Three questions I'm asking

1. **Where am I leaning on my own understanding?** Usually it's the place where I'm anxious and trying to control the outcome.
2. **Where do I need to acknowledge Him?** Decisions I haven't prayed over yet.
3. **What is one step I can take this week?** Small, specific, and doable.

### Number our days

> "So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom."
>
> <cite>Psalm 90:12 (ESV)</cite>

A week is a short stretch of days. Wisdom starts with remembering that my time is a gift, not a guarantee.

### Work as for the Lord

> "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men."
>
> <cite>Colossians 3:23 (ESV)</cite>

That's the posture I want for Monday morning: whatever is on my desk, do it as worship.

## Closing thoughts

Before you start the week, take five minutes. Read one passage, ask one question, and write down one step. Then trust God with the rest.
