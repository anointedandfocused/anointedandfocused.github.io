## Key takeaways

- Feeling unqualified is not the same as being unprepared.
- God usually gives the next step, not the whole map.
- Faithfulness today is how I prepare for tomorrow's calling.

## My reflections

A lot of us wait for a feeling of readiness before we obey. I've learned the feeling rarely shows up first. More often, I take the step, and the confidence comes while I'm walking.

> "For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them."
>
> <cite>Ephesians 2:10 (ESV)</cite>

Notice that the good works were *prepared beforehand*. God is not waiting to see if I'm impressive enough. He has already gone ahead of me. My part is to walk in what He has set before me.

### What to do while you wait

Waiting does not have to mean standing still. It can mean serving where you are, learning, praying, and working faithfully on whatever is in front of you.

> "Commit your work to the Lord, and your plans will be established."
>
> <cite>Proverbs 16:3 (ESV)</cite>

## Closing thoughts

If you're sensing a nudge from God that feels bigger than you, take the next small step of obedience today. You don't have to see the whole path.
